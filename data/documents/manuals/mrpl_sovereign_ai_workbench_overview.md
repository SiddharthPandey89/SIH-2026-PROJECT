# MRPL Sovereign On-Premise Agentic AI Workbench

## Brief Description

The MRPL Sovereign On-Premise Agentic AI Workbench is a secure, offline-first artificial intelligence platform designed for Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL). It helps authorized users interact with organizational knowledge, documents, reports, and operational information through locally hosted AI models and agentic workflows.

The workbench is designed to keep sensitive refinery and petrochemical information within the organization's controlled infrastructure. It supports local document processing, knowledge retrieval, conversational assistance, task planning, and report generation without depending on external cloud AI services.

## Theme: Smart Automation

The platform advances smart automation by combining local language models, document intelligence, retrieval-augmented generation, and task-oriented AI agents. Users can ask questions, search approved knowledge sources, summarize documents, upload operational files, and coordinate multi-step tasks from a unified workbench.

## Key Capabilities

- Secure interaction with locally hosted AI models
- Agentic planning and execution for multi-step tasks
- Search and retrieval across manuals, standard operating procedures, and past reports
- Document upload and local knowledge-base ingestion
- Context-grounded answers with relevant source information
- Conversational history and task tracking
- Generation of reports and other useful work outputs
- Support for text documents, PDFs, images, and scanned operational material through the appropriate processing pipelines
- System and network monitoring for offline and on-premise operation

## Operational Value for MRPL

The workbench can help teams access information faster, reduce repetitive knowledge-work, improve consistency in routine tasks, and support informed decision-making. By bringing AI capabilities closer to the organization's data and workflows, it can contribute to safer, more responsive, and more efficient refinery and petrochemical operations.

## Sovereignty and Security Principles

- Data remains within MRPL-controlled infrastructure.
- Sensitive documents are processed locally wherever supported.
- Access to organizational knowledge should be limited to authorized users.
- AI responses should be grounded in approved internal sources for operational use.
- Generated outputs should be reviewed by qualified personnel before they are used for safety-critical, maintenance, production, or compliance decisions.
- The workbench is an assistance and automation system; it does not replace established operating procedures, expert judgment, or formal approvals.
